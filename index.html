DO / DOES

We use do and does to make questions and negative sentences in the Simple Present Tense.

DO

We use Do with I, we, you, they for questions and negative sentences.

I do
We do
You do
They do
use Do

DOES

We use Does with he, she, it for questions and negative sentences.

He does
She does
It does
use Does
KEY NOTES
  • 1

    Do + I / we / you / they

    Digunakan untuk pertanyaan dan kalimat negatif.

    • Do you like coffee?
    • We do not play games.
    • They do not live here.
  • 2

    Does + he / she / it

    Digunakan untuk pertanyaan dan kalimat negatif.

    • Does she like pizza?
    • He does not have a bike.
    • It does not rain often.
  • 3

    Untuk pertanyaan, letakkan Do/Does di awal kalimat.

    • Do you study English?
    • Does he play football?
  • 4

    Untuk kalimat negatif, tambahkan not setelah Do/Does.

    • I do not like spinach.
    • She does not watch TV.
  • 5

    Setelah Do/Does, gunakan kata kerja bentuk dasar (verb 1).

    • ✅ Does she play the piano?
    • ❌ Does she plays the piano?

More Examples

  • • Do they like chocolate?(Apakah mereka suka cokelat?)
  • • Does it have a tail?(Apakah itu punya ekor?)
  • • We do not watch TV.(Kami tidak menonton TV.)
  • • He does not eat meat.(Dia tidak makan daging.)
  • • Do you go to school?(Apakah kamu pergi ke sekolah?)
  • • Does she live near here?(Apakah dia tinggal di dekat sini?)

Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Does you like pizza?
  • ✅ Do you like pizza?
  • ❌ Do she live here?
  • ✅ Does she live here?
  • ❌ She does not likes coffee.
  • ✅ She does not like coffee.
  • ❌ Do they plays games?
  • ✅ Do they play games?
Remember! After do/does, use the base verb (verb 1)!
